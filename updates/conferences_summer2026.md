  <p class="conference-meta">MACRO 2026, Reading, United Kingdom, July 2026</p>
  <p>

<div class="conference-entry">
  <img class="centerimage" src="/assets/images/PLACEHOLDER.jpg">
  <h4>Alessio Capobianco and Gustavo Darlim &mdash; TALK OR POSTER TITLE</h4>
  <p class="conference-meta">Paleo4Alps, Bolzano, Italy, July 2026</p>
  <p>
  Alessio and Gustavo presented their ongoing work in <a href="https://www.unibz.it/en/events/paleo4alps-2026">Paleo4Alps </a> - Joint Meeting of the Italian, German, Austrian and Swiss Paleontological Societies, which took place in Bolzano, Italy between 19 and 23 July.
  In Gustavo's talk, he addressed systematica and diversification dynamics of Notosuchians (Cretaceous terrestrial crocodyliforms from Gondwana).
  Meanwhile, Alessio presented a talk with the title "Total-evidence tip-dating reveals macroevolutionary dynamics in deep-sea squaliform sharks".
  </p>
</div>

<div class="conference-entry">
  <img class="centerimage" src="/assets/images/PLACEHOLDER.jpg">
  <h4>Wenjie Zhu &mdash; Challenges in genomic species delimitation: insights from European Luciola fireflies</h4>
  <p class="conference-meta">MASAMB 2026, Cambridge, United Kingdom, September 2026</p>
  <p>
  Wenjie presented her work at the <a href="https://www.mrc-bsu.cam.ac.uk/events/mathematical-and-statistical-aspects-molecular-biology-masamb-annual-meeting-2026">Mathematical and Statistical Aspects of Molecular Biology (MASAMB) meeting </a> in Cambridge, United Kingdom on September 7th.
  Her talk, "Challenges in genomic species delimitation: insights from European Luciola fireflies," revealed unrecognized diversity in this group.
  The study also showed how heterogeneous genomic data can be integrated for species delimitation and highlighted the challenges of reconciling results across delimitation methods.
  </p>
</div>
